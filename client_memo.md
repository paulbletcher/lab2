## What I recommend

**Prioritize bundles and open talks for The Lord of the Rings.** Its middle
result is $11.73 per budget dollar across three films; it ranks second across
all years and first from 2000 onward. **Star Wars** leads across all years at
$13.40 across six films. **Indiana Jones** follows at $10.89 across four.
For releases from 2000 onward, The Lord of the Rings, **Ice Age** ($8.26), and
**The Avengers** ($6.87) lead.

Keep five individual targets: **The Good, the Bad and the Ugly** and
**Pulp Fiction** for the highest ticket sales per budget dollar; **Schindler's
List** for the highest audience rating among the five; **Spirited Away** as the
strongest individual film from 2000 onward; and **Forrest Gump** for the highest
ticket sales among the five.

**Talent:** Steven Spielberg ($13.25 across eight films) and first-billed
Mark Hamill ($29.91 across three) lead across all years. Peter Jackson
($6.73 across six) and Elijah Wood ($11.73 across three) lead from 2000 onward.
Each ranking requires at least three films.

**Deal note:** Consider a modest premium for The Lord of the Rings over the
other two bundles, conditional on comparable terms covering all three analyzed
films. Its weakest observed film earned **$9.37 per budget dollar**. Set a price
ceiling only after confirming rights, fees, and expected viewing value.
